Close — never hire on this call

The verdict below comes from the marks above. Make the call, and the script for it fills in from what they actually said.

How the verdict is scored

Every answer is worth 0 to 4 points. Green is always 0. The middle option is usually 1 (sometimes 0 — "okay, not awesome"). The red option is 3 for a big red flag, 2 for a real one, 1 for a small one. Points add up.

  • 👍 Bring them on — 0 to 2 points.
  • 🤔 In the middle — 3 to 5 points.
  • 👎 Thumbs down — 6 or more, or one disqualifying answer.

The big ones (3 points red): didn't watch the video · blames others for their situation · got defensive on the belief flip · evasive about money they've owed · not working and won't commit to hours · under 15 hours around a job · no idea how we get paid, or clueless about chargebacks · no idea how we get clients, or can't fund any leads. Most of these mean they didn't really watch the video.

Disqualifying on its own (4): not working and couldn't sell you on why.

Real but not fatal (2): can't name anything hard they've pushed through · never done 30 days of anything.

Small — flagged, mostly a conversation (1): wants to quit tomorrow with no runway · a stretch without a check would be rough (and if they commit to a plan, it drops to 0; evasive about a plan, it goes to 3) · partner against it.

Questions on the hidden side of a branch don't count. The verdict is a recommendation — you make the call, and whichever way you go, the answers that drove it are listed so you can say them out loud.
