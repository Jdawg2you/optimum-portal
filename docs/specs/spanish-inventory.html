The number that ambushes translation projects. Those sections total ~4,400 words, but the portal holds ~17,400 words of English prose overall. The gap is interface states — toasts, error messages, empty states, the coaching card, modals, the pre-licensed flow. You only see them when you’re in them, so they never make it onto an inventory, and then an agent hits a validation error in English on a Spanish form. Budget ~7,500 for Round 1, not 4,400.

Options, cheapest first: YouTube auto-translated captions today; a written Spanish overview page she can send instead; or she records a Spanish overview herself — best, and costs her an afternoon.

What I need from her

  • Review of Round 1 wording before it ships — roughly an hour for 4,400 words.
  • Her call on Usted vs. Tú. Formal reads professional; informal reads like a team. It has to be one or the other across everything, and it’s a brand decision, not a translation one.
  • Which industry terms stay English. My default: lead, underwriting, carrier names, product names, rating classes.
