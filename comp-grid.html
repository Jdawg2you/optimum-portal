How to read this

  • Every number is first-year commission as a percentage of the annual premium. A 110% on a $1,200 premium is $1,320.
  • Your FFL level is not your commission. The same 110 contract pays 110% on one product and 55% on another.
  • The column tells you more than the cell. Two carriers can look identical at 145 and be twenty points apart at 90.

Before you plan around a number

  • Rates differ by term length, issue age and graded status. The InstaBrain block is the clearest example — see the full InstaBrain grid.
